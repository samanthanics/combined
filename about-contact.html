Organizational Communication

    About Us and Contact

    Meet Combined Group

    Combined Group helps organizations communicate with clarity inside the workplace and with the people they serve. We are a small, structured team that treats every client message as an important piece of business.

    Company introduction

    We are a team of four University of Makati students working as a consultancy for the Organizational Communication subject. Our company studies how information moves through an organization and then designs simple, reliable ways to improve it. We work with small and medium businesses, student organizations, and community groups that want clearer messages and better teamwork.

    Our name and identity

    What Combined means

    Combined describes how our company was formed. It joins four members, Cummins, Balmaceda, Delcano, and Noora, into one team. It also describes our work, which is bringing people, messages, and departments together so they act as one.

    Our logo

    The logo places the first letter of each surname, C, B, D, and N, in the four quadrants of a navy circle. A gold cross joins the quadrants at one center point, which shows four members combined into one company. Navy blue stands for trust, champagne gold for quality, and white for clarity.

    Nature of the business

    Combined Group is a professional services business in the corporate communication industry. We sell expertise and structured work instead of physical goods. Each engagement begins with listening to the client, continues with analysis and planning, and ends with practical deliverables and training that the client can use right away.

    Products and services

    Internal communication audit

    A review of how information flows between leaders and staff, with a report of strengths, gaps, and recommended fixes.

    Corporate messaging and brand voice

    Guidelines for tone, key messages, and standard templates that keep every announcement consistent.

    Communication training workshops

    Sessions on business writing, effective meetings, listening, and presentation skills.

    Stakeholder and crisis communication plans

    Ready-to-use plans that show who speaks, what is said, and through which channel when something goes wrong.

    Event and presentation coaching

    Preparation support for launches, town halls, and client presentations.

    Documentation and process guides

    Clear written procedures, checklists, and handbooks that help teams work the same way every time.

    Company background and purpose

    Our company began in 2026 when our group set out to turn classroom theory into a working business model. We chose Max Weber's Bureaucratic Theory as our foundation because it shows how structure, rules, and records make an organization dependable.

    Our purpose is to show that communication is a skill that can be designed, measured, and improved. Through our services, we want organizations to spend less time correcting misunderstandings and more time reaching their goals.
