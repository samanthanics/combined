    Company background

    Combined Group was established in 2026 in Makati City as a corporate communication consultancy. It was created by a team of University of Makati students who saw that many organizations struggle with unclear messages, slow approvals, and teams that do not share information.

    The company brings together the study of organizational communication and practical business support. Our team combines specialists in internal communication, client relations, and operations and finance management, so that each client receives complete service from planning to delivery.

    The company is a fictional business created for the Organizational Communication subject. It is designed and presented as a real business so that our group can apply the theories we studied.

    Mission and vision

    Mission

    Make communication clear, fair, and effective

    We help organizations build communication systems that connect their people, protect their reputation, and turn information into action. We do this through honest advice, practical tools, and training that clients can keep using after our project ends.

    Vision

    Workplaces where every voice is heard

    We see a business community in the Philippines where teams share information openly, leaders explain their decisions, and communication is treated as a core professional skill.

    Organizational commitments

    These are the promises we make to our clients, our team, and our community.

    To our clients

    We deliver work on schedule, explain our recommendations in plain language, and keep client information confidential.

    To our team

    We assign roles fairly, give feedback regularly, and support every member's professional growth.

    To our community

    We share communication skills with students and small organizations through free learning sessions.

    To quality

    We review every deliverable against written standards before it reaches a client.

    Organizational principles

    Our principles come directly from Weber's Bureaucratic Theory and shape how we work every day.

    • 1
      Defined rolesEvery position has a written description of its duties and limits.
    • 2
      Clear chain of commandEach member reports to one superior, and approvals follow the chain.
    • 3
      Documented decisionsImportant decisions, instructions, and results are recorded and filed.
    • 4
      Equal and fair treatmentPolicies apply to everyone, and members advance on skill and performance.
    • 5
      Consistent proceduresStandard workflows and checklists keep results reliable across projects.
    • 6
      Open feedbackMembers may propose improvements to any procedure through a regular review.
